## Introduction

```markdown
Hi, I'm Ozan. I live in Kastamonu, Türkiye, and build free, open-source software independently.

**What I work on**

- **Linux tools for the Omarchy desktop.** [OmaStudio](https://github.com/ozdil/omarchy-omastudio) edits 16-bit medium-format RAW files, [PolyCodex](https://github.com/ozdil/polycodex) translates PDFs while keeping their layout using local models, and [QuickNews](https://github.com/ozdil/quicknews) is an ad-free news reader. Smaller components cover notes, network scanning, ad blocking, benchmarks and themes.
- **[FOSSWire](https://fosswire.org)**, an open-source software and hardware news site in 17 languages, without ads or trackers.
- **Open source in local government.** I coordinate open-source work and the GitHub organisation of Kastamonu Municipality.

**What sponsorship pays for**

Hosting and build costs for FOSSWire and ozanozdil.com, test hardware for the Linux tools, and the time to fix bugs, answer issues and keep the projects working with new Omarchy and Arch releases.

None of these projects carry ads or tracking, and they stay free whether or not you sponsor. Thank you for considering it.

---
```
